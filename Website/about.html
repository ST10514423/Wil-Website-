About Adventure Escape SA

Who We Are

Adventure Escape SA is an outdoor adventure organisation dedicated to creating exciting and memorable experiences in nature.

Our activities provide opportunities for people to explore beautiful environments, challenge themselves and create lasting memories with friends, family and colleagues that will last a life time.

★

Our Story

Adventure Escape SA was created to encourage people to step away from everyday routines and experience the beauty of the outdoors,family,and connections. From hiking and kayaking to camping and team-building activities, our goal is to make adventure accessible and enjoyable to you, your friends and loved ones.

◈

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide safe, exciting and meaningful outdoor experiences while promoting teamwork, exploration and appreciation for South Africa's natural environment.

What We Value

Adventure

Encouraging people to explore beyond their comfort zones.

Safety

Making responsible and safe outdoor experiences a priority.

Nature

Respecting and protecting the natural environment.

Community

Creating experiences that bring people together.
